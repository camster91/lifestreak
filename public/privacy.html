Privacy Policy — LifeStreak

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

Developer: Ashbi Design (cameron@ashbi.ca)

Summary

LifeStreak is a private habit and goal tracker. We collect no personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

LifeStreak does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. No account, analytics, tracking, advertising, crash reporting, or data-collecting third-party SDKs are required.

Data Storage

Your progress, settings, habits, notes, service hours, and reading list are stored locally on your device. Your data does not leave your device and we cannot access it.

Permissions

LifeStreak may request local notification permission for reminders. This permission is optional and can be disabled in device settings.

Data Export

You can export your data from Settings as a JSON file. You control where the exported file is saved or shared.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cameron@ashbi.ca.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted within the app and on this page.
